# KatBot

## What is the KatBot ?

By its official name, the *Magnificent Tool Of Destruction* is a weapon that is used by the character Lowain in the great fighting games "Granblue Fantasy Versus" and "Granblue Fantasy Versus Rising". It is visually inspired by the character of Katalina.
I am making it as a props for a cosplay of Lowain, with the daggers being the controllers.
